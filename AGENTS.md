## SMART goals and plans

For every new or materially revised goal, outcome, roadmap, project plan, experiment, or milestone, write down: (S) the specific result, scope, and owner; (M) an observable acceptance test or metric, baseline (`unknown` if unmeasured), target/threshold, and evidence source; (A) capacity, dependencies, assumptions, and what is actually controllable; (R) the parent purpose and opportunity cost; and (T) a real deadline or decision date with review cadence. An event-triggered option needs an activation condition and dated periodic review, not an invented due date. Keep targets, forecasts, and commitments distinct. At review, compare actual evidence with the target and record the next decision. Routine microtasks may inherit the parent outcome; important legacy plans migrate when next revised.
